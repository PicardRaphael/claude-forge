# ADR 0007 — Neutral review packet

Status: Accepted

## Decision

Red Team receives a versioned packet containing the neutral brief, factual constraints, evidence, hypotheses, unknowns, sources and authorized comparable outcomes; private Product persuasion and ideation history are excluded.

## Consequences

Independent review is less anchored. V1 neutralization is template/section-based and must be supplemented by future semantic checks only after evaluation.
